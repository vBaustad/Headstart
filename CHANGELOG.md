# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **The Camping 101 reminder says it once.** "Mining is done: hand it in to Yarr Hammerstone" came again at every loot: the quest log answers "not complete" for a moment while it updates, and that made the reminder forget it had said it. Now it's said once, and again on coming into Kharanos at most every two minutes.

- **Bank mats works again.** A check added in this release stopped it at the first stack with "the bank closed before the rest went" while the bank was open: it asked the game whether you were at a banker, and on Forever the bank opens without saying so. It now looks at the bank window itself.

- **The new character window closes once you click Apply to this character** (it stays, and says why, if you're in a fight).

- **Settings, redesigned.** Each page opens with its icon, what it's for, and who the settings on it are for ("Every character on this account", "Every Paladin"), saved as you change them. The menu has an icon per page and a dot that is green while something on that page is on. Settings come in cards with an icon; a feature with one switch has it in the card's header, and the rows under it dim while it's off. Under every setting is the first line of what it does, so you can read the page without pointing at anything (the (i) still has the whole text). "Find a setting" at the top of the menu searches every page by name and description, and picking a result opens its page with the setting lit up.

- Dun Morogh: the bears for **Thick Bear Fur** (Stocking Jetsteam) are now east of Kharanos, on the way to Steelgrill's Depot, where all ten furs in five logged runs dropped. RestedXP's loop south of Kharanos never gave one, and a run lost about 10 minutes looking.

- **Treacherous Cold is dropped when another addon accepts it.** The route skips it, but Leatrix Plus's quest automation (and the like) takes every quest Father Gavin offers. Headstart drops it once, and says so; take it again and it stays. A quest you read and accept by hand is left alone.

- Thelsamar crafting stop: says the Camping 101: Blacksmithing hand-in in Kharanos isn't worth a trip back. A run that hearthed back for both Camping 101 hand-ins spent about 10 minutes on 540 XP.

- **Buy later and vendor restock no longer error at a vendor.** Both asked the game for an item's price in a way only the old Vanilla interface has, which Forever doesn't load ("Shopping.lua:116: attempt to call a nil value" at a vendor selling a weapon you were saving for).

- **Restock: ammo for warriors and rogues too, and throwing weapons.** The ammo row shows for every class that shoots, and each keeps its own number (a hunter 1000, a warrior or rogue 200 to begin with); before, a warrior with a bow bought a hunter's 1000 arrows with no way to change it. With a throwing weapon in the ranged slot, more of the same one is bought.

- **The Headstart window opens on QoL & settings while the routes are off** (on Routes while they're on), its Settings pages are built the first time you open each one so the window opens at once, and the better-gear window has Headstart's own look, its icon framed in the item's quality colour, and stays where you drag it.

- **Level splits: a "± level" column.** How much each level alone won or lost against the best run's same level, "-0:40" in green for 40 seconds faster, beside the level's own time. The live row shows it too. Its own switch under Level splits.

- Save as route: a death skip says exactly where you died, "Die right here (28.8, 83.3)", with a pin on that spot, and which Spirit Healer that brought you back at. Where you die decides which healer you come back at, so our general wording ("in the cave") isn't enough there. It is the death you made for that step, even if you did the next stop first.

## 0.10.0-beta1

- **Save your run as your own route** (This run, Save as route). A run that followed our routes becomes a route of our own steps, as we wrote them (level gates, conditions, waypoints, notes), in the order you played them; steps you skipped are left out, and what you did that our route doesn't have gets a step of its own from the log (a quest stop at each NPC, objectives finished, trainers with what you learned, vendors with what you bought, flights, the hearthstone, death skips, and grinds: at least 10 kills over 3 minutes in one spot right before your next stop). It is split into one part per zone you levelled in, chained so RestedXP moves on by itself, and named by levels, zone and race ("1-6 My route: Dun Morogh (Dwarf/Gnome)"), never by character. One set per race, Dwarves and Gnomes sharing: saving again from any character of that race replaces that set and your edits to it; other races' sets and our routes stay. Edit it in the Routes list like ours (drag steps, edit any line, delete); RestedXP lists it under Headstart Launch, My routes. **Delete my route** (This run) takes it away again; your run log stays.

- Death skips: Headstart now accepts the resurrection at the Spirit Healer itself when the death was a death skip. RestedXP only does it while its death-skip step is still the active one, and several end as soon as you arrive (the Kharanos ones end on entering Kharanos, where the healer stands), so sometimes nothing happened. Any other death is still left to you.

- **Your place in a route survives an update.** RestedXP remembers the step you're on by its number, so when a Headstart update adds or removes steps before it, a /reload puts you somewhere else (a run was moved past the trogg hand-ins, the Dark Iron spies and Farsen straight to the walk to Loch Modan). Headstart now remembers the step itself and puts you back on it, and says so in chat.

- Dun Morogh: **Camping 101: First Aid** (270 XP): taken from Eric at the campfire right after training First Aid, Linen Bandages to 20 as you go, handed in to Thamner Pol at the level-10 visit to the Distillery. And the grind before the death skip at Brewnall now aims at 5800/6500 XP (Bellowfiz's 875 then makes 10) instead of just "level 9", which let a run come out of Bellowfiz 51 XP short.

- Dun Morogh: when Bellowfiz leaves you just short of 10 (within 300 XP), a step says to kill a mob or two on the way to the Thunderbrew Distillery first, so the level-10 training steps don't skip themselves. A run came out of Bellowfiz 51 XP short.

- **Trainer settings:** every spell in the list has its icon, learned yet or not.

- RestedXP's "go to" icon (the red smudge in front of travel lines) is a clean grey map pin while the routes are on. And no more "Cast Find Minerals" steps (or other tracking spells): a buff addon keeps those up.

- **Level splits:** the XP/hr line also says how far into the level you are, "XP/hr: 15.5k   1.2k / 2.8k".

- **The run log takes half the room.** Standing still, the position samples between the first and the last on one spot are left out (one kept every 30 seconds, and the last one before you move or log out), so play time and every move come out the same. Logs from before are thinned once the same way at login: measured on a real log, 46,760 samples become 23,981 with the play time unchanged. **Run logs** (Settings, Route) shows how much room every character's log takes, deletes one character's log (it asks first; this character's starts again from now), can delete at login the logs of characters you haven't played for a number of days (off until you set it), and deletes the quest scan's results.

- Dun Morogh, Never Saddle on Quality: the leopard pelts are marked optional (kill the Elder Snow Leopards you meet at the quarry, don't go looking), and the step goes away at the point where you'd go back to Rudra instead of staying in the window into Loch Modan. The trip back to Rudra still only shows with all six pelts.

- **Camp HUD reads every station.** The tent says "Rested XP" instead of "You", the fish bowl "All stats +5%" instead of "All", the lute gives three rows (armor, all stats, resistances), the mana well "Mana +40/5s", and the long names are cut to what you'd say ("Attack Power", "Crit"). It looks as Campfire's does now (white for a stat with no colour of its own, the colour taken from any word so "Melee Attack Power" isn't grey, a stronger shadow, less dimming while the numbers are a few seconds old), and its campfire icon is Headstart's own, so it shows without Campfire installed.

- **Performance and taint pass.** The gear sim keeps its answer while you point at an item (the tooltip asked five times a second), and the better-gear check only looks at new items after a loot, and waits for a fight to end. The flight bar draws when it moves a pixel, not every frame. Dropdown menus no longer leave hidden rows behind, the bar planner's search only redraws the list, and macros are looked up once per Apply to this character instead of once per button. "Open Headstart" in the game's Settings no longer closes Settings (that is blocked from addons and dropped unsaved changes). A skill-up in a dungeon no longer errors (the chat text is secret there). Edit Mode's fallback waits out a fight and stops when the account's layouts are full. A menu left open closes when a fight starts, a spell is never placed while you carry a button on the cursor, and the bank stops moving items the moment the banker closes.

- **The better-gear window** grows with its text, so the lines no longer run into the buttons, and an armor-only piece says "+4.0% toughness" without "+0.0% DPS" in front. A piece that is better and takes nothing from you (a belt with 6 more armor) is offered for any real gain; the 2% only holds back trades, like less DPS for more armor. Two upgrades for one slot (two pairs of boots from one quest) give one window, for the better one, and each window is checked against what you wear right before it shows, so it never offers something worse than what you just put on; one already up closes when your new gear beats it. A shield or held item is never offered while you wield a two-hander (the tooltip still says what it would do). Equip pressed in a fight puts the item on as soon as the fight ends; pressed while the game says no (casting, eating, stunned), the offer comes back instead of being lost.

- **Gear sim** (Settings, QoL, Gear & rewards: Show it in item tooltips): every piece of gear you point at says what wearing it would do for you - "+4.2% DPS, +1.0% toughness (Retribution)" - worked out from your own attack power, crit, spell power, health and armor with the item swapped in for what it replaces. Your role comes from the talent tree with the most points (or what your class levels as before that), or set it yourself. It reads "Equip:" lines too (spell power, healing, crit, hit, attack power, mana per 5). Spell power counts for a melee paladin (seals and Judgement) and a melee shaman (shocks); a weapon's "Chance on hit: N damage" counts at about one proc a minute; and against a two-hander you wield, a one-hander comes with the best off hand in your bags and an off hand (a shield) with the best one-hander in your bags - or an empty main hand - as the game's own comparison does ("..., with Seedcloud Buckler"); a shield no longer looks like an upgrade next to your two-hander. A levelling estimate: set bonuses, on-hit effects and trinkets aren't in it. The upgrade notice uses it as well ("for you, over ...: +4.2% DPS"): anything that fills an empty slot, any gain that costs you nothing, and a trade from 2% better.

- **The interface is the account's, not a class's.** Chat windows and tabs, Edit Mode, game settings, raid frames, the camera and which bars show are saved once, by the newest Copy this layout on any character, and every class gets them - set up a paladin main, and a new rogue has the same chat without copying anything on a rogue. Bars stay per class. A saved plan holds bars only, so a level-1 character's defaults never replace your interface. The newest interface you had copied before becomes the shared one.

- **"Set up layout" is now "Apply to this character"** (and the planner's "Save and set up" is "Save and apply"): it puts the saved layout on the character you're on, which "set up" didn't say. Spell buttons: Only spells (the default for a class you set up now), Spells with a question mark until learned, or Macros for every spell - mouseover spells and your own macros are macros whichever you pick, profession spells never. A downranked button's macro casts its rank ("Holy Light(Rank 2)"). Character setup and the planner show how many of the game's 30 character macros the layout takes.

- **The bar planner, round two.** Forms and stealth: a "Bar 1" choice plans the bar your Stealth, Cat or Bear Form, or each warrior stance swaps in (read from the game's own form data). The list has three tabs: Spells now also holds Attack and your weapon spells (Throw, Shoot, Shoot Bow ...), Professions has what a profession puts in your spellbook (Find Minerals, Smelting, Cooking, Basic Campfire, Disenchant ...), and Macros has your account's and this character's macros, with a button to open the macro window and make more. Long key names fit their button ("Mouse Button 5" is M5). Fixed: a bar's planner buttons could stay on screen after closing it, when a bar before it wasn't shown. Character setup copies Attack and the weapon spells from a main too (they were dropped), and setting up a character again - after changing the plan mid-levelling - keeps the macros you made on it and only clears Headstart's own question marks.

- **The routes can be switched off as a whole** (account-wide, for every character): no Headstart routes in RestedXP, and nothing that works off them (items kept or bought for a route, the death-skip release, the Camping 101 reminder). Off for a new install, so Headstart can be just the QoL addon; an account that already played the routes keeps them on. Takes a /reload.

- **QoL & settings, reorganised.** The window's first page is now "QoL & settings", with its own menu down the left: Camps & travel, Bags, Vendors, Group, Reminders, Gear & rewards; Character setup and Trainer; and Route settings. Each page has a heading and a line on what it's for, and every page lists one setting per line instead of a two-column wall, with each label kept clear of its switch, slider or box (cut short with an ellipsis rather than running under it). Every scrolling list has a real scrollbar now: grab the handle and drag it, or click above or below it to move a page.

- **Plan your bars on your real bars.** The planner draws the plan over your own action buttons, where Edit Mode put them, so you see it as you'll play it - drag spells straight onto them. It's a picture laid on top (the real buttons are untouched until you Save and apply), and it goes when the planner closes or a fight starts. "As a grid" is still there for bars you haven't switched on, and opens by itself when a bar addon draws its own buttons. Every button shows its key binding top right, as the real button writes it, empty or not. A downranked button shows its rank big (R3); the learned-at level is no longer on the icons. The planner sits above the Headstart window, which steps out of the way while you plan on your bars.

- **Trainer defaults: "If I can afford it" and a 2 silver reserve** for a class you haven't set up yet, so a new character never trains itself down to nothing; "Always" spells are still learned whenever the gold is there. The reserve is set in silver now. New on the Trainer page: "Always, up to level" with a "Set to Always" button, for the spells you want the moment you can, and "All: If I can afford it". A class you already set up keeps what it had.

- **Quality of life in Settings.** Headstart is also the home of the YippYapp quality-of-life features now, the ones that help on any character whether you follow a route or not. - **Mail to my alt** (Settings, QoL): for bag space - name an alt and a "Send to <alt>" button under the mail window sends what the bank would take (crafting mats no profession of yours uses, or all or none; recipes you can't learn yet) plus anything you put on your own list, twelve to a letter. If you like it happens as the mailbox opens (off by default: the postage, 30 copper a stack, is your money); hold Shift as it opens to send nothing. Never soulbound or quest items; of the mats and recipes, nothing the route still needs or AutoFeed's food (your own list goes as you made it); and nothing to yourself, so the same settings work on the alt. The name is kept per realm and faction. /headstart mail does the same as the button.

- **Vendor restock** (Settings, QoL): at a vendor who sells them, your class reagents, your ammo and your own list are bought back up to the number you keep. The reagents come from the game's own spell data and are only bought while you know a spell that takes them, and only for the top rank you know (a druid with Rebirth 5 doesn't buy Maple Seeds). Hunters get the best arrows or bullets the vendor sells for their ranged weapon that they can use (1000 by default). Shift-click anything else into "Add to my list" with a count. "Keep at least (gold)" stops it before it eats your money; hold Shift as you open a vendor to buy nothing. Chat says what was bought, for how much, and what the money didn't stretch to.

- **Party quests** (Settings, QoL): accept quests and escorts shared by guildies and friends in your party (or anyone in it, or nobody), and if you like, share every quest you take - not only the route's. Hold Shift as the quest opens to be asked. Leatrix Plus's "Block shared quests" declines them before Headstart sees them.

- **Quick group** (Settings, QoL): into a group for a kill and out again without typing. A small bar with Invite (your target) and Leave group, both also on key bindings (Key Bindings, AddOns, Headstart) and /headstart inv, /headstart leave. Whoever whispers you "inv" (the word can be changed) gets an invite - guildies and friends by default, or anyone, or nobody - when you're solo or lead a group with room. Group invites from guildies and friends (or anyone, or nobody) are accepted at once; hold Shift as one comes in to be asked. Whispers can't be read during the game's chat lockdown, so none are answered then.

- **Reminders** (Settings, QoL): at a ding (and at login), what your class trainer has for you now and about what it costs - the prices are remembered from your trainer visits - and talent points you haven't spent. A durability warning when your most worn piece drops under 25% (once, until you repair) and a "repair while you're here" when you come into a town under 50%; both lines can be set. And a screenshot at every ding.

- **Things you can make yourself** (Settings, QoL, Reminders): you know the recipe and carry the mats for sharpening stones or weightstones, wizard or mana oil, or bandages, but have none in your bags - chat says the best one you can make and how many ("you can make 3 Coarse Sharpening Stone from your bags, and you have no sharpening stones - open Blacksmithing"). Stones only for the weapon you carry (blades sharpened, blunt weapons weighted). Once, and again after you've had some and run out. Each kind can be switched off, and "remind when I have fewer than" set.

- **Better gear** (Settings, QoL): an item in your bags that beats what you wear in that slot - by the gear sim (above) - gets a chat line and a small window with an Equip button. Only what you can wear (the game's red text says no), grey to green by default, at least 2% better (anything for an empty slot, or a gain that costs you nothing, at any size), once per item and again after a ding. Rings and one-handers are measured against the weaker one you wear, a two-hander against main hand and off hand together.

- **Bank** (Settings, QoL): crafting mats and recipes you can't use yet go to the bank by themselves when you open it, one stack at a time, and chat says what went. Crafting mats: "Not for my professions" (the default) keeps what a profession you have crafts with - ore for a miner, linen for a tailor - and banks the rest; or all of them, or none. Recipes go when you haven't got the profession or need more skill; one you can learn now stays, and one you already know is left for the vendor. Quest items, dynamite and other things you use, anything the route still needs and AutoFeed's food and water never go. Hold Shift as you open the bank to keep everything; a "Bank mats" button under the bank window (and /headstart bank) does it whenever you like. It stops when the bank is full or closes.

- **Campfire icon** (Settings, QoL): a campfire on screen that tells you where you stand with camps. Grey with "No camp benefit" when nothing is going on; it lights up while a camp is within about 100 yards. With the Campfire addon showing its own campfire, Headstart's stays hidden, so there is only ever one.
