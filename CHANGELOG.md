# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **All features: a front page for the settings.** Every feature as one card with its name, one line and its switch, so nothing has to be found before it can be turned on. Click a card for its settings.
- **The menu is grouped by what you are doing:** Combat, Levelling, Loot & bags, Social, Interface. Death recap and Characters have pages of their own. Pages with nothing switched on are dimmed.
- **Features are called by their names** (Death recap, Restock, Upgrade alerts ...), and every setting's text was rewritten: a short name, then one plain sentence that says what it does. The search also finds a feature by other words for it ("died", "altoholic").
- **XP bar preview.** Draw the bar as it would be with no rested XP, rested, or rested with completed quests, so the colours can be set without waiting for the real thing.
- **New in this version** on the All features page: the newest features as cards, until you hide them.
- Every window, popup and chat message was reworded the same way; the flight timer, the swing bars' edge and Headstart's name in chat follow the accent.
- Copy interface now also carries whether the game's damage meter is on, and whether it resets in a new instance.
- **Copy interface carries RestedXP's look,** not only where its windows are: the theme (Headstart's RestedXP skin is one), scales, font size and text colours. RestedXP keeps these per character, so a new character used to get its defaults.
- **Interfaces have names.** A saved interface is called Main, and you can save more (Healer, Tank) under Character setup, Interface: Save as, Rename, Delete, and which one new characters get. Each character is on one. Copy interface is now Save interface.
- **Every character on an interface keeps it up to date,** not only the one it was first saved on: change something on any of them and it is saved when you leave Edit Mode or log out. Only a real change is saved.
- **Changed on another character?** At login the setup window asks: Update puts the change on this character, Keep this character's saves this one over it. Until you answer, nothing is saved over the change.
- An interface saved by an older version lacks the damage meter and RestedXP's look. Apply interface and the first-login window say so; saving it again fixes it. The window also says how old the saved interface is.
- **First login: Apply both.** With an interface and bars both saved, one button puts on both, with one reload. Apply interface alone no longer ends the window: after the reload it is back with the bars still to do.
- **Loot feed: Cards.** A second look for the feed: each line on a dark plate with a bigger icon, and what the stack sells for to a vendor at the right. Look and Vendor price are under Loot feed; the price can be shown on plain lines too.
- **Headstart has its own icon:** an ember H on a dark disc, on the minimap button, in the addon list and on the All features page.
- Fixed: the Trainer page listed the wrong spells. It showed talents no trainer teaches (Riposte, Blade Flurry, Hemorrhage...) and left out spells you start with, such as Sinister Strike and Eviscerate, whose later ranks you do pay for. It now lists what the class trainer teaches, at the level of the first rank you pay for, with how many ranks. The trainer data is from build 70334 and no longer holds the ranks of talents with several points, which also made the trainer reminder count them.
- **Auto-train: First rank only.** A fourth choice per spell: the spell itself is learned, its later ranks are left for you. For a spell you start with, nothing is bought.
- RestedXP skin: the line under the header no longer runs across the class icon.
- Redirect is gone from the rogue's spells (trainer list, bar planner): it is in the client's tables and not in the game.
- **Trinket buttons: your own look, with a working preview.** Size, spacing, opacity, side by side or stacked, when they show (always, in combat, out of combat), cooldown numbers, tooltips, which click uses the trinket and which lists your trinkets, the list on hover, and where the list opens. The preview at the top of the card is two buttons you can hover and click, in three states: ready, on cooldown, and a swap waiting for the fight to end. Reset position is new too.
- **Trinket auto-swap, finer.** A used trinket stays on while its effect is still on you (taking it off used to end it), and for as many seconds as you set. A trinket in your bags counts as ready with 30 seconds left, since equipping one costs 30 anyway; both are settings.
- Lighter: Character setup keeps its two and a half thousand profession spell names as one text (some 230 KB less held), the trainer data builds only your class at login, your bars are read again only when a button really changed (not for every macro that shows another spell), and putting on gear redraws the swing bars and trinket buttons only for weapons and trinkets.
- Fixed: trinket buttons set to show only in combat (or only out of it) could show at the wrong time until the next fight.
- **Spell data checked against foreverchanges.pro for all nine classes** (build 70338). The trainer list no longer holds a hunter's pet's abilities, procs named like spells (Chilled, Clearcasting, Impact), Season of Discovery leftovers (Aspect of the Viper, Shadowfiend, Black Arrow) or what is left of Classic's passive talents; it now has what Forever trains that Classic made a talent (Blessing of Kings, Omen of Clarity, the later ranks of Holy Shock and Trueshot Aura). The bar planner lost the same wrong spells. Which spells are talents, and the level each can first be taken, now come from Forever's own trees (Holy Shock 30, not 40; Blessing of Kings is trained, not a talent), read from the site's talent calculator by `tools/fetch_talents.py`. `tools/check_spellbook.py` runs the comparison again.
- Named interfaces, from a second review, before anyone has used them: a chat window's channels are read whole (leaving a city left a hole that cut the list, and the cut list would have gone to every character); RestedXP resizing its own windows, or channels in another order, no longer counts as "changed"; a character saves nothing until its screen has been looked at once after the reload; what Apply didn't put on a character (a part switched off, or a UI suite's) is not saved from it; Undo takes the character off the interface; the first interface saved is the one new characters get whatever it is called; the setup window marks a character done only when something was applied.
- Trainer data: a rank that comes with a talent point is not listed as trained (priests were told to train Holy Nova), and a rank is one spell (Immolation Trap and Lightning Shield were listed twice). Auto-train's First rank only no longer buys a later rank when the trainer's rank text hasn't loaded.
- Trinkets: "Swap in when ready within" stops at 30 (past it two trinkets swapped every second); buttons shown only in or out of combat are drawn as things are when they appear.
- A macro edited in place is remembered in the profile again.
- Settings sliders are compact (a track and the number), so two fit on a row. The mouse wheel moves one step.
- Swing timer: the game is asked about your target's range again when a fight starts and when the target changes. It has one switch for the range check and Blizzard's bars share it; whether this is why the out-of-range colour sometimes stayed off is not confirmed.
- **Bar planner:** an Items tab with the usable items in your bags (hearthstone, potions, bandages), and items, macros and spells can be dragged onto a planner button from your bags, the macro window and the spellbook. A macro with the question-mark icon shows the spell it casts, as on your real bars. Esc, or a click beside the buttons, puts down what you carry instead of closing the planner.
- **RestedXP skin (Headstart look):** steps no longer show through under the guide's title, and the title's field grows for a two-line name.
- **Macro helper: many more ready-made macros**, 11 to 18 a class, gathered from the Classic class guides that agree with each other: stance macros, mouseover heals and interrupts, pet control, Stealth and forms that stay on, Pick Pocket openers, downranked spells on a modifier. Each is listed only for a character whose class has the spells it needs, and gets a short name of its own. Left out on purpose: anything that needs /focus or names an item you may not carry.
- The button on your bag for the Characters window is a small round mark in the money bar: hover it for every character's gold, click it for the window.
- **Macro helper: build a macro a line at a time.** Pick a spell, when it should cast (in stealth, not in a stance, in combat, with Shift held ...), on whom (the friend or enemy under the mouse, yourself, your pet), and whether pressing again keeps it on - then Add line. Switches put the icon line, /stopcasting and /cancelform in and take them out. The text itself can be written in. New rogue presets: Stealth, then Sap, Cheap Shot, Ambush or Garrote on one button.
- **Macro helper: your macros for the class** under the presets. They are remembered on the account, so a new character of the same class is offered them, greyed until made. Export gives them as plain text to paste anywhere; Import takes such a text in. Click one to load it, change it and Save changes; the x on it deletes it (it asks first). "Or else" hangs a spell on the last /cast line after a semicolon: `/cast [stealth] Sap; Stealth`.
- **Macro helper: presets as a gallery.** The ready-made macros are tiles with the spell's icon and a name, four across, in place of a dropdown; point at one for its text, click it to fill in the choices on the right.
- **Talent spells the planner didn't know:** Riposte, Cold Blood, Adrenaline Rush, Preparation, Ghostly Strike, Inner Focus, Shadowform, Silence, Divine Favor, Sanctity Aura, Amplify Curse, Curse of Exhaustion, Demonic Sacrifice, Fel Domination and Feral Charge can now be planned on a bar and used in macros.
- A dropdown menu closes on Esc, on its own button again, and on a click anywhere else.
- **Resource bar: hide the spend flash** (Combat bars; off until you turn it on). Blizzard's energy, rage and mana bars keep the part you spent up as a pale block that fades; with this on the bar is at its new value at once.
- **The account's interface has one source character and stays current.** Copy interface makes a character the one new characters get their interface from (it asks first when another one is). On that character, what you change in Edit Mode, chat and settings is saved again as you leave Edit Mode and as you log out. Saving another character's bars no longer replaces it, so a new hunter doesn't get the healer's screen. The first-login window does the interface first, then the bars, and says whose interface it is.
- **Swing timer: a Slim look**, and the new default: low flat bars close together, a nearly black back, each hand in a quiet colour with a brighter line at the front of the fill, the hand's name in small letters. Pick Slim or Classic (the look from before) under Swing timer, Look, Preset.
- **Bar planner opens on the stance or form you are in.** A warrior's bar 1 is always a stance's bar; the planner opened on the page no stance shows, with buttons the warrior never sees. It follows as you change stance, and Attack is drawn with your weapon's icon as on the real bar.
- **Swing timer: one kind of bar.** Headstart now only restyles Blizzard's own swing bars - texture, colours, text size, outline, a bright line at the front of the fill - and no longer draws bars of its own beside them. Size, position, title, time and when they show are Edit Mode's, where they already were; the game times them itself. A preview you start and stop keeps sample swings running while you change the look. Whoever used Headstart's own bars is moved over.
- **Settings two to a row.** A setting is its name at the left of its half and its control at the right, and the page uses its full width. What a setting does is shown when the mouse rests on its row; what a card is about, when it rests on the card's head. Dropdowns are one width, sliders keep a row to themselves, and rows that were only words are gone.
- **Combat bars** is the page for Blizzard's swing bars and its resource bar (Hide the spend flash moved there from Bars & windows).
- **A new look:** warm charcoal with an ember accent instead of grey and blue. Pick another accent, your class's colour, or the old look under All features. The RestedXP and damage meter skins follow it unless you chose a colour for them.

- **Fixed: Apply took your hearthstone off the bars.** An item you carry and put on a button yourself always goes back on (the "Items" switch is only about a main's items this character hasn't got), and what you changed on your bars a moment before pressing Apply is saved into the profile first.

- **A profile remembers your bars as you change them.** Drag a spell to another button while levelling, put a potion on one: a few seconds later it is in the profile - there when you come back from another profile, and on your next character of the class. Spells planned for later levels keep their buttons. A switch of its own (on). And the card at the top of Character setup now says in one line where this character stands and the one thing to do next, if anything.

- **Profiles** (Headstart, Character setup). A warrior tanks and deals damage; a druid heals and claws. A profile is your bars under a name ("Tank"); make another ("DPS") and switch between them: the profile you leave is saved as your bars are that moment - what you moved by hand is remembered - and the one you go to is put on. Only the buttons that differ change, and a mount or toy button no profile holds is left alone. A profile can have **its own interface** too (off unless you turn it on for that profile): which Edit Mode layout is active, which extra bars show, where RestedXP's windows are - so a healer's screen comes with the healer's bars. Switch from the settings, a right-click on the minimap button, a key ("Next profile") or `/headstart profile Tank`. The planner plans the profile you are on. **Your two specs**: the profile you were on in each is remembered, and when you change spec Headstart asks whether to switch to that spec's profile (or switches by itself, or does nothing). And the **Character setup page is four short tabs** now - Profiles, Bars, Interface, New characters - under one card with the profile you are on, Plan bars and Apply.

- **Bar planner: Save and apply takes away the question marks you no longer ask for.** Chose "Only spells" (or fewer levels ahead) after question-mark macros were made? They come off their buttons and are deleted - the spell itself goes in where you know it. And setting up again only ever deletes macros that are exactly Headstart's own: a macro of yours that begins the same way (a "!Stealth" one, one from the macro helper) is left alone.

- **Fixed: Headstart never recognised its own question-mark macros.** The game gives a macro's text back with a line break at its end that was never written, and every check compared the text exactly. So a question mark was not swapped for its spell when you learned it, the planner showed it as a "?" instead of the spell, a plan saved from such bars kept it as a macro of yours, and setting up again could make it a second time. The text is read without what trails it now, everywhere.

- **Setting a character up again no longer takes your interface back in time.** A character that has been set up before is asked every time, and the answer under Enter is **Bars only**: the saved bars go on, and Edit Mode, the bars you switched on, chat and game settings stay as you have them now. "Everything" still puts the saved interface back, and says whose it is and how old.

- **Character setup carries RestedXP's windows over**: where the guide window, the arrow and the item and target buttons are, and how big. RestedXP keeps those per character, so a new character had them back in the middle of the screen. Saved with Copy interface (or Copy this layout) and put on with the rest of the interface; a switch of its own.

- **Character setup says when your choices aren't on your bars yet.** Changing a choice (question marks to "Only spells", say) changes nothing on a character until the layout is put on it again - the question marks stay. The page now says so, in gold under the saved layout, until you Apply to this character.

- **Character setup: talents are talents.** A spell a talent gives (Blade Flurry, Mortal Strike, Premeditation) counted as a level-1 spell, so a new character got a question-mark button for something thirty levels and twenty talent points away. Such a spell now has the level its row in the tree can be reached, is marked "talent, 30+" in the planner, and never waits as a question mark: it keeps its button and goes in when you learn it. **How many levels ahead** a spell waits as a question mark is now a setting (10). The planner's spell list is in the order you learn them, with your race's spells after the class's. Spell levels are regenerated from the newest client data.

- **Macro helper** (`/headstart macro`, and "Macro helper..." on the bar planner's Macros tab; part of Character setup): the macros every class ends up wanting, made by ticking what the button should do. Pick a spell, then: pressing again doesn't turn it off (Stealth, a form, Auto Shot, a wand), cast on who the mouse is over first, Alt casts on yourself, stop what you're casting first, leave your form first, switch to a stance first (a warrior's Charge from any stance). The macro's text is shown as you tick. And a short **ready-made** list for your class: a rogue's Pick Pocket with an opener, a warrior's Charge-or-Intercept in one button, a druid's heal out of any form, a healer's mouseover heals. It writes macros of this character; it presses nothing for you.

- **Character setup: Copy this layout asks before it replaces a saved layout** - a plan you made, another character's bars or this one's from before - and says what it would replace. And **Copy interface** and **Apply interface** are buttons of their own at the top: chat windows, Edit Mode, game settings, which bars show and the camera, without touching one action button.

- **Bar planner: the question marks Headstart made are spells, not macros of yours.** A plan saved from bars that had Headstart's waiting macros on them (for spells not learned yet) kept them as macros: they were listed among your macros, and would never have been swapped for the spell when you learned it. They are read as the spells they stand for now, and plans already saved that way are put right at the next login.

- **Bar planner: the saved plan wins on a character that never had it put on.** Open the planner on, say, a level 15 of a class you already have a plan for: it shows the plan, not whatever the game dropped on that character's bars as it levelled (which it used to, button by button). On a character set up from the plan, and where there is no plan yet, it starts from the bars as before. Where the two disagree a tab at the top says which one you're looking at - "Saved plan" or "My bars" - and a click swaps it.

- **Every window, one click away**: right-click the minimap button (or Headstart in the addon list) for a menu of every window of ours that is on - characters, chat log, loot log, instances, settings. Each also has a key of its own (Key Bindings, AddOns, Headstart), and `/headstart menu` opens the menu.

- **Characters** (Headstart, QoL, Bank & mail; off until you turn it on; `/headstart alts`): what your other characters carry, have banked and own, seen from the one you're on. **Gold**: hover the coins in your bag for every character's gold and the sum (`/headstart gold` says it in chat). **Tooltips**: an item's tooltip says which of your other characters has it ("Duplo: 4x, bank 12x"). **The window**: your characters with level, gold and when last played; pick one for what it has - laid out as a bag is, icons with their count and their quality's colour, sorted into crafting materials, recipes, gear, consumables and the rest - and its currencies, or all of them to find who has a thing. Soulbound items, quest items and the hearthstone are never noted; food, drink and grey items are hidden unless you ask for everything. A small button on your bag opens it. Gold is noted the moment it changes, bags and currencies a few seconds after the last change (never in a fight), the bank while it is open - and each character has to have played once with this on.

- Fixed: "you can make 2 item 3530 from your bags" - an item the game hadn't loaded yet was named by its number. The line now waits a moment for the name.


- **Instance tracker: the copy's ID** (as Nova Instance Tracker tells them apart): while you're in a dungeon, the ID of the copy is read off the first creature or object the game lets us read (your target, what the mouse is over, a nameplate, what you loot), and shown in the log. Back in the same copy without a reset is the same instance however long you were out; another copy is a new one even when nobody saw a reset. Where the game keeps those IDs secret it is told as before (the same dungeon, the same leader, within the minutes you set). `/headstart instances id` says what could be read where you stand. Also: a reload inside a dungeon is no longer noted as having gone back in, the log has headings and the date, and a row keeps its highlight under the mouse.

- **Welcome window** (`/headstart start`; shown once on a new install, a few seconds after the first login): not everyone wants all of Headstart, and what people want isn't one dial from little to much, so it asks four questions, each answered by itself. **Routes of your own** (off / on: your runs recorded as you level and saved as routes you make yourself, played back in RestedXP), **On your screen** (nothing / essentials / everything), **Does things for you** (nothing / for friends: invites and shared quests, no gold and no items / everything: restock, training, banking and screenshots too) and **New characters** (Character setup off / on). Under each question a line says what your answer turns on, before anything changes; a question you leave unanswered stays as it is. Three quick picks answer them all at once: Minimal, Recommended, Everything. What another addon of yours already does is left off and named. It takes the place of the Get started page, which is gone from the window; a Welcome button at the bottom of the settings opens it again.

- **Whisper sound: a file of your own, and a shorter list** (Headstart, QoL, Chat): write where a sound file of yours is under the game's Interface folder (AddOns\whisper.ogg) and it is the whisper sound - any sound you have, from any UI you used before. The game's window clicks and rustles (backpack, menu, page turn, coins and the like) are out of the list; one you had picked still plays.

- **Talent window: the see-through slider and the size buttons can be read**: with the window seen through, they stood grey on whatever was behind it. Each now has a dark plate of its own, and brighter words.

- **XP bar: a new look** (Headstart, QoL, XP bar): your XP as a gradient from one colour to another (each yours to pick, or one flat colour), a clean seam where it meets the completed quests' part (the spark shows only against the empty bar; the Smooth texture is now a sheen of our own over the whole bar, where the game's bar picture faded each part at its own end), a line of words over the bar as well as under it, numbers with their thousands apart (4,585 / 6,500), and new first words: XP left to the level, your percent once the finished quests are handed in ({pctq}), time this level and this session, time to level with XP an hour, completed and rested percent. Words and colours you set yourself are kept.
- **XP bar: blue means rested** (Headstart, QoL, XP bar): our bar was blue whether you were rested or not, which read as rested when you weren't. Your XP is now purple with no rested XP and blue while you have some, as Blizzard's bar does it, with a colour of its own for each (XP colour, XP colour rested). The rested XP now reaches on from where you are, as on Blizzard's bar, instead of being a block after the completed quests: it sits behind them and shows where it reaches past them. Colours you picked yourself are kept.

- **RestedXP's pins show above the game's quest markers on the world map** (with routes on). They sat on the lowest marker layer, so a quest area or another marker over the same spot hid where the route wanted you. Other addons' pins are untouched.

- **Classic ding information** (Headstart, QoL, Reminders; on unless you turn it off): "You have gained 19 hit points. Your Strength increases by 1." - the lines Classic wrote at a level-up, which Forever leaves out. Health, mana and each stat that went up, in the game's yellow, a moment after the ding.

- **Run logs and scan results are out of the account's saved file.** That file is read in whole at every login on every character, and the run logs of every character you had played were in it: some seventeen megabytes in memory on an account with nineteen logs. Now a character's run log is kept in that character's own saved file (`HeadstartRunLog`), read only on that character. A log still in the account's file - of a character not logged in since this version - is packed to text the first time you log in (a moment longer on that one loading screen, and a line in chat): a third of the size, checked to unpack to exactly the same log, and it moves to its character's own file the next time that character logs in. *This run*, Save as route and the level splits work as before; another character's packed log still shows in the list of logs with its size and when it was played. What `/headstart scan` found is in the window addon's saved file (`HeadstartScanDB`, in `Headstart_Options.lua`), read only when that addon is. If you read these from the saved files with tools of your own: a character's log is in `WTF/Account/<account>/<realm>/<character>/SavedVariables/Headstart.lua`, and a packed one is `{ packed = { track = { "return {...}", ... }, ev = { ... } }, ... }`, each piece plain Lua.

- **Free while off.** A part of Headstart you haven't turned on now costs nothing at all: its frame isn't made, it listens for no event, no timer runs and no hook does work - and all of that goes away again when you turn it off. Before, most parts listened always and looked at their setting each time. With everything switched off Headstart listens for one thing (other addons loading); it was ninety-two event registrations, twenty-seven of them events that fire many times a minute, and five timers. A new install as it comes went from 120 registrations and five timers to 33 and none. This is counted at every change by a test that logs in with everything off and fails if anything is left running, so it stays so. Settings still take effect at once, without a reload.

- **Lighter while on, too.** The camp icon's clock runs only while a camp buff with a timer shows (it ran every frame for everyone, the icon being on by default). The loot feed and the flight bar run nothing per frame. The instance tracker listens for experience only inside an instance, and the level splits and the XP bar redraw only when the second shown changes. The chat log keeps a line as three numbers, not a table. What crafts with what (some 2,300 small tables read in at every login) is one number an item. `/headstart perf` shows what Headstart costs - memory, and time out of each frame, by the game's own profiler - beside the addons that cost the most.

- With a feature turned off: the flight timer no longer times and learns flights in the background, the instance tracker turned off inside an instance ends that run, trinket swapping turned off puts back what you wore, and with *Release to the graveyard* off the Spirit Healer isn't accepted for you either. A chat log or loot log left from when its feature was on is dropped at the next login.

- **Loot feed and loot log** (Headstart, QoL, Loot; off until you turn it on, also in the welcome window): what you just got as a few lines on screen that fade - items with their icon in their quality's colour, money, experience, reputation; the same thing again adds to its line. Choose which of them show, the lowest item quality, how long a line stays, and where it sits. The **loot log** (`/headstart loot`) lists every item and reputation gain with its time, and this session's sums: money looted, what your loot is worth at a vendor, experience - and each an hour. Read from the game's own chat lines, in any language; a line the game keeps secret is passed over.

- **Chat log** (Headstart, QoL, Chat; off until you turn it on, also in the welcome window): every line your chat windows show is kept for the character - earlier sessions too - and a window of its own shows it large: every line in its colour, as text you can mark with the mouse and copy. Pick one of your chat windows or all of them, and search. **The chat itself gets its old text back**: a login or a reload leaves the chat windows empty, and the last hundred lines each of them showed are put back (how many is yours to set), so you can scroll up in chat as if you had never left. A button on the chat's button strip (over the chat menu's) opens the log, and so does `/headstart chat`. How many lines are kept is yours to set (1000), as is whether they are kept between sessions; a line where each session began is in the log. The combat log's window isn't kept, nor a line the game keeps secret.

- **Character setup is a tab of its own**, beside QoL and Levelling at the top of the window: the bar planner, and a main's bars, macros and interface on a new character (it was the page *Bars & setup* under QoL). It can be switched off whole, and so can Levelling: the tab stays, and shows only its switch and what it is. **On a new install Character setup is off**: no window on a new character's first login, and nothing is ever placed on your bars, until you turn it on. An account that already had Headstart keeps it on. It is an addon of its own now, the folder `Headstart_Setup` ("Headstart: character setup"), loaded only while it is on - turning it on loads it there and then, turning it off takes a /reload to free the memory. Headstart is three folders now: `Headstart`, `Headstart_Options`, `Headstart_Setup`.

- **What happened, when you die** (Headstart, QoL, Reminders; off until you turn it on, also in the welcome window): a panel under the Release button. At the top the killing blow - its picture, its name, who, and how much, with crit and overkill. Under it the last things that happened to you, each a bar as long as the hit was hard: the hardest in large gold letters, crits and crushing blows marked, heals and dodges between them. Then what you took this fight by spell. The hits are noted as they land (the game says how much, not by whom); who and with what comes from the game's own damage meter once the fight is over. Nothing is shown or said while a fight is on. Where the game keeps the numbers secret it says how many hits were hidden. `/headstart death` shows the last one again; Preview in the settings shows a made-up one.

- **Old run logs are cleared away, the ones still used are kept.** At login, the run log of a character not played for 30 days is deleted - except this character's, a fastest run's (one that holds the best time to some level among its class's runs in the level splits) and one a route of your own was saved from. It says so in chat when it does. It used to keep every log forever unless you set a number of days; the number is still yours to set (Headstart, Levelling, Route settings; 0 keeps them all). A route saved before this version doesn't say whose log it was made from, so every log of that race is kept until it is saved again.

- **The window is an addon of its own now: Headstart comes as two folders**, `Headstart` and `Headstart_Options` ("Headstart: window" in the AddOns list). The settings window and the route editor - some two hundred kilobytes of code - are loaded the first time you open the window (`/hs`, `/headstart`, the minimap button) and not at every login; someone who never opens it never has it in memory.
